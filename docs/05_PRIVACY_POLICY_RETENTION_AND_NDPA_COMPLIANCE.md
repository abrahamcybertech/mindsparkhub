# MindSpark Hub Official Platform: Privacy Policy, Retention Schedule and NDPA Compliance

## 1. Compliance Statement

MindSpark Hub operates in strict compliance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** and Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) guidelines. This framework establishes our lawful basis for data processing, data minimization standards, and individual data subject rights.

---

## 2. Lawful Basis for Processing

| Activity | Data Collected | Lawful Basis (NDPA 2023 Section 25) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public Contact Inquiries | Name, email address, inquiry text | Consent (freely given via unticked checkbox) | Responding to public requests for guidance or information |
| Volunteer / Mentorship Applications | Name, email, phone, skills, area of interest | Legitimate interest & explicit consent | Evaluating alignment with MindSpark Hub youth mentorship programs |
| Staff & Admin Accounts | Name, official email, salted password hash, TOTP secrets | Contractual necessity & legitimate security interest | Securing administrative access to institutional records |
| Minor Outreach Photography | Seminar imagery (FGC Wukari SSI 1.0) | Documented school and parental consent | Demonstrating verified civic and educational impact |

---

## 3. Data Subject Rights Procedure

Every Nigerian citizen whose personal data is held by MindSpark Hub possesses the following statutory rights under the NDPA:

1. **Right of Access:** Citizens can request a portable copy of all personal records associated with their email address.
2. **Right to Rectification:** Prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** Deletion of applicant data upon request, subject to legal compliance.
4. **Right to Object to Processing:** Immediate cessation of communication.

### Submitting a Data Protection Request
- Official Contact: `privacy@mindsparkhub.org` (Data Protection Officer)
- Statutory SLA: Acknowledged within 48 hours; resolved within 14 calendar days.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion Schedule

| Record Classification | Retention Period | Destruction / Purge Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| General Contact Inquiries | 12 months after resolution | Cryptographic deletion from database; audit log entry recorded |
| Volunteer & Mentor Applications | 24 months from submission date | Permanent purge; anonymized statistics kept for institutional reporting |
| Administrative Audit Logs | 7 years (statutory requirement) | Kept in immutable append-only storage; non-repudiable audit trail |
| Minor Imagery under Takedown Request | Immediate (within 1 hour) | Immediate file unlink from object storage and database status revoked |

---

## 5. Cookie and Analytics Policy

MindSpark Hub operates a **privacy-first zero non-essential cookie architecture**:
- **No Third-Party Trackers:** No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or commercial advertising beacons are embedded on this site.
- **Essential Cookies Only:** The only cookies used are cryptographically signed, secure, HTTP-only session cookies necessary for authenticated administrative users to access the dashboard.
- Because no non-essential cookies or third-party trackers are utilized, annoying cookie banners are unnecessary, respecting visitor privacy by design.
